A PRIVATE PERSONAL ASSISTANT

A little more room
in your day.

Metis helps turn the plans in your conversations into appointments you can review, alongside your everyday reminders and priorities.

How appointments work ↗

A personal project by Tim Kok. No public signup.

01 / NOTICE

Pick up the plan.

With permission, Metis checks new Gmail messages and text from linked WhatsApp chats. For chat plans, she looks for your agreement to join.

02 / REVIEW

Keep the context.

Review the source excerpts, date, time, location and possible calendar conflicts. Correct details or ignore the proposal.

03 / APPROVE

You make the call.

Only after your approval does the iPhone app save the event to your Metis calendar. Later changes need another approval.

CLEAR BOUNDARIES

Connected with your permission.

Gmail access is read-only. The appointment integration does not send email or WhatsApp replies. AI can misunderstand a message, so every calendar change is yours to review.
